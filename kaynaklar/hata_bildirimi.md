# Kaynak hataları bildirimi

İki ayrı İngilizce ileti: biri Dag Hammarskjöld Kütüphanesi'ne (veto listesi), biri
scprocedure.org'a (Tablo 4). Her madde yalnızca toplantı kaydından (S/PV) doğrulanmış bulgulara
dayanır; kayıtta paragraf numarası varsa birincil atıf odur, PDF sayfası yanında verilir; paragraf
numarası olmayan kayıtlarda documents.un.org PDF sayfası kullanılır (basılı sayfa değil). Tarihler
oylamanın fiilen yapıldığı takvim günüdür. Dayanaklar kayıt bazında `duzeltmeler.csv`'dedir.
Gönderimi proje sahibi yapar; gönderim kaydı en alttaki bölümdedir.

**İletişim kanalları** (10 Ekim 2026'da bakıldı; iletiler gönderilmedi):
- Dag Hammarskjöld Library: kütüphanenin çevrimiçi iletişim formu
  https://www.un.org/en/library/contact-us (soru, ayrıntı, ad, e-posta, meslek, kurum alanları);
  Ask DAG (https://ask.un.org) yalnızca SSS veri tabanı ve aynı forma yönlendiriyor; canlı sohbet
  https://www.un.org/en/library/chat-us. Sayfada genel e-posta adresi yok. Kütüphane dış
  araştırmacıların sorularını yanıtladığını ama bunun asıl görevi olmadığını yazıyor.
- scprocedure.org: ana sayfadaki "For inquiries, or to receive notice of new updates, please
  contact: scprocedure@earthlink.net". Site Loraine Sievers tarafından yürütülüyor ("exclusively
  responsible for its contents"); Sam Daws kitabın ortak yazarı. İleti bu adrese, iki yazara
  hitaben gönderilebilir.

## 1. İleti: Dag Hammarskjöld Library (Security Council veto list)

Subject: Possible discrepancies in the Security Council veto list

Dear colleagues,

Veto Atlas (Veto Atlası) is a reference dataset that documents the vetoes cast in the Security Council from the primary sources (meeting records, draft resolutions and the official lists).
While reconciling the Dag Hammarskjöld Library's Security Council veto list with Table 4 of
*The Procedure of the UN Security Council* (scprocedure.org) for this dataset, we noticed a few points at which the list appears to differ from the meeting
records. We compared two presentations of the list:

- (A) the static page https://www.un.org/Depts/dhl/resguide/scact_veto_table_en.htm (copy of 10 October 2026);
- (B) the list in the Research Guide, https://research.un.org/en/docs/sc/quick/veto (page updated 23 September 2026; copy retrieved 10 October 2026).

The eight points below (numbered 1–8) were each checked against the meeting record itself. Where a record has numbered
paragraphs we cite the paragraph, with the PDF page of the documents.un.org file in
parentheses; otherwise we cite the PDF page. We would be grateful if you could look at them,
and we may of course have misread something.

## 2. İleti: scprocedure.org (Table 4)

Subject: Possible discrepancies in Table 4 (vetoes, version of 18 September 2025)

Dear Ms Sievers and Mr Daws,

Veto Atlas (Veto Atlası) is a reference dataset that documents the vetoes cast in the Security Council from the primary sources (meeting records, draft resolutions and the official lists).
While using Table 4, "Vetoes of draft resolutions and proposed amendments cast in formal Security
Council meetings, 1946 to present" (version of 18 September 2025,
https://www.scprocedure.org/_files/ugd/54a569_8c1ef1fee8ca4d6ab09b7fb516b55a22.pdf) for this
dataset, we compared each entry with the Dag Hammarskjöld Library's veto list
and, where the two differed, with the meeting record. We noticed five points (numbered 1–5) at which the table
appears to differ from the record. Where a record has numbered paragraphs we cite the paragraph,
with the PDF page of the documents.un.org file in parentheses; otherwise we cite the PDF page. We
may of course have misread something.

| # | No. in Table 4 | As listed | Possible discrepancy | Meeting record |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 43 | 13 Dec. 1949, overall vote 8-2-1 (Argentina) | **9-2-0.** At the request of Argentina the Canadian draft S/1431 was voted in two parts. The first part (all but the final paragraph) received 9 in favour, including Argentina, and 2 against (Ukrainian SSR, USSR). The 8-2-1 result with Argentina abstaining belongs to the final paragraph only, which is No. 44. | [S/PV.456](https://undocs.org/S/PV.456) (no paragraph numbers), PDF pp. 36–37. |
| 2 | 96 and 97 | 20 Feb. 1961 | **21 Feb. 1961.** Both votes came after midnight. The table already dates the vetoes at the 911th (No. 94) and 1443rd (No. 109) meetings by the day after midnight, so 21 February would be consistent with that practice. | [S/PV.942](https://undocs.org/S/PV.942): the meeting opened on Monday, 20 February 1961 at 9.15 p.m. (PDF p. 3); it was suspended at 12.45 a.m. and resumed at 1.10 a.m. (after para. 111, PDF p. 24); the first veto (amendments to the first preambular paragraph, 8-3-0) is at para. 139 (PDF p. 29); the meeting was suspended again at 2.25 a.m. and resumed at 2.55 a.m. (PDF p. 33); the second veto (United States amendment, 7-3-1) followed para. 175 (PDF pp. 34–35); the meeting rose "on Tuesday, 21 February, at 4.20 a.m." (after para. 247, PDF p. 48). |
| 3 | 198 | 17 Sept. 1995 | **17 May 1995.** | [S/PV.3538](https://undocs.org/S/PV.3538) (no paragraph numbers): Wednesday, 17 May 1995, 6.15 p.m. (PDF p. 1); vote on S/1995/394, PDF p. 6. |
| 4 | 224 | 8 July 2015, 11-1-4 (total 16) | **10-1-4.** | [S/PV.7481](https://undocs.org/S/PV.7481) (no paragraph numbers), PDF p. 7: in favour 10; against: Russian Federation; abstaining: Angola, China, Nigeria, Venezuela. |
| 5 | 243 | 13 Dec. 2021, 13-2-1 (total 16) | **12-2-1.** | [S/PV.8926](https://undocs.org/S/PV.8926) (no paragraph numbers), PDF p. 5: in favour 12; against: India, Russian Federation; abstaining: China. |

The Veto Atlas dataset has now been published at https://vetoatlas.com
(DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23326362). Overall vote counts for a number of records were taken from Table 4
and are attributed to it; where we could, we verified them against the meeting records, and the
corrections listed above come from that process. The table itself is not redistributed. If you would
prefer a different form of attribution, or would like us to remove any material derived from
Table 4, please let us know and we will do so promptly.

Table 4 has been very helpful to us, and we would be
glad to share the full reconciliation file if that would be useful.

With kind regards,

Nuh Mehmet Yıldız, independent researcher · iletisim@vetoatlas.com
